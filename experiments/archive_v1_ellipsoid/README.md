# Archive: v1 egg-shaped skull (superseded)

These results come from dataset v0.1.0, whose "skull" was a smooth deformed ellipsoid with a few bumps (no orbits, nasal
opening, cheekbone arches, teeth or mandible). It was replaced by an anatomically-styled skull template (dataset v0.2.0)
because the egg shape was unrealistic. The numbers here are kept for the record and are NOT comparable with v0.2.0 results.
Interrupted ablation runs (abl_*) were stopped mid-training when the generator changed; they are incomplete.
